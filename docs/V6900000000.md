# TravAI Elite v6.9B — Reliability + Recovery

Adds the Recovery Center and a governed offline recovery pipeline.

## Capabilities
- health scan and readiness score
- pre-upgrade immutable snapshots
- SHA-256 snapshot manifests
- snapshot verification and tamper detection
- staged restore plans
- explicit human approval before restore
- post-restore verification
- runtime/restart history and recovery recommendations

## Safety
Restore is never automatic. Unverified snapshots are rejected. No arbitrary shell capability is added. The service remains localhost-first.
